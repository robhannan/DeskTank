What is this?

DeskTank is a desktop aquarium in progress: a pixel-art falling-sand tank that will run as your wallpaper, screensaver or a click-through overlay on macOS, Windows and Linux. This page is its sand and water simulation core, compiled from Rust to WebAssembly and running in your browser at a fixed 20 ticks per second. Fish, plants and the real renderer come later.

What is this?

DeskTank is a desktop aquarium in progress: a pixel-art falling-sand tank that will run as your wallpaper, screensaver or a click-through overlay on macOS, Windows and Linux. This page runs its simulation and its GPU renderer, compiled from Rust to WebAssembly: WebGPU where your browser has it, WebGL2 otherwise, at a fixed 20 ticks per second. Fish and plants come later.

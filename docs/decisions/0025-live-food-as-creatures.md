# D25: Live foods are creatures

**Live foods are creatures** (2026-11-20). Live bloodworms (later brine shrimp and earthworms) are small creatures of a `live_food` species, not powder: they move by the creature systems, are hunted and swallowed whole by species whose diet holds the matching food material, and die into a cell of that material. They come from the Food palette group a pinch at a time, never breed and sit outside the population cap under their own limit (§2.4 *Live food*).

# D21: 2.5D depth

**2.5D depth** (2026-11-19). The picture stays 2D, but the tank has discrete depth bands for movement and collision, and later for occlusion (plant cells in front of a creature drawn over it): plants are no longer solid in every band, so fish slip past them and reach food lodged inside them (§2.4). Full 3D (perspective, a camera, 3D models) remains a non-goal. Occlusion (part 2, 2026-11-20) needs no second grid pass or depth buffer: the grid is drawn once, sprites follow back to front, and each sprite fragment checks the cell under it in a per-cell band texture, dropping itself where a plant cell in a nearer band covers that texel (its shape is evaluated as the grid shader does). One grid pass and one sprite draw per frame, whatever the number of creatures or bands; per-band plant layers would cost up to eight grid passes, and splitting the grid in two works for one depth only. A subtle depth cue fades creatures and plant cells towards the water colour with depth.

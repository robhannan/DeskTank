# D27: Prefab devices

**Prefab devices** (2026-11-21). Equipment is a device instance in a world device table (id → kind, anchor, facing, parameters, state), built like bodies and plants; its footprint cells hold its id, so powders and creatures still collide with it, it is drawn as a hand-drawn sprite (silhouette traced from a reference photo, then house shading) and scooping any of its cells removes it whole (save format v11; v10 saves load). Filtering comes in later increments: intakes capture suspended particles into media, load clogs the filter but flow never falls below about 30% (unattended hosts can't rinse), Rinse clears it, media matures with bacteria and in simple mode improves waste breakdown and water quality, and hard rinsing or replacement sets maturity back; the nitrogen chemistry stays in E1. A HOB's intake tube is in the back depth band; other filter bodies and tubes are solid in every band. The filter outlet becomes the internal filter's nozzle and the bubbler becomes a prefab (DV7); old saves convert ([devices](../spec/devices.md) *Migration*). Intakes suck in fry and small shrimp unless the intake guard is on (the sponge filter is fry-safe). Facing comes from the nearer wall with a ghost preview and a flip control; the inspect card shows device status, and later Rinse and the guard. Idle devices cost nothing per tick and the current is rebuilt only on change ([devices](../spec/devices.md), milestone `MD — Devices`).

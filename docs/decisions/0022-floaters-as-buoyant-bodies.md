# D22: Floaters are buoyant bodies

**Floaters are buoyant bodies** (2026-11-20). A floating plant (duckweed, frogbit) is held at the surface and carried by the current as one object, built on the rigid-body motion (D17, T4c), not by a cell rule that pins floating leaves to the top water row. The plant table stays its owner (species, health, growth, budding, cutting, death); a per-plant raft holds only its motion (sub-cell offset and velocity), so cells growing onto it or cut from it need no re-anchoring. A rule would have kept leaves at the surface but let a plant's roots and leaves part when the current carried some cells and not others; as a body the whole plant moves together, rests by its leaves' buoyancy, falls back when lifted out, and stops at the glass. Rafts are saved (format version 7) and hashed. Epiphytes on moving bodies are settled in D24.

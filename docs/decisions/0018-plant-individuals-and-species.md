# D18: Plants are individuals of data-defined species

**Plants are individuals of data-defined species** (2026-11-18). Each plant is distinct from every other, even of the same species, with its own age, health and size; species set environment preferences, growth speed, size, hardiness and appearance in TOML (§2.7). **Varied growth forms** (branching stems, rosettes and runners, carpets, floaters, epiphytes and mats) and **dynamic leaf appearance** (age and health shading, colouration in strong light, pearling, optional sway) are wanted, not stretch goals (T6b–T6d).

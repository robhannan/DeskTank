# D24: Epiphytes and mats stay on the grid

**Epiphytes and mats stay on the grid and are torn off when a body moves away** (2026-11-20). An epiphyte (anubias, java fern) or mat (java moss, christmas moss) growing on a rigid body (rock or driftwood) does not ride the body if it is unglued or knocked away: rigid bodies translate and rotate (T4c), and a plant can span multiple bodies or static scenery, so riding a rotating body would shear cell-aligned leaves and require body-attachment state in saves and body-split logic. Instead, hardscape should be settled or glued before planting on it; when a body moves away or is scooped out, any rhizome or mat cell that no longer touches its support loses its hold and falls off as dead plant matter (cascading through outer mat layers, and killing epiphyte leaves left without a rhizome). No extra save state or per-tick body coupling is needed.

- T4c rigid-body physics (D17 revised): bodies keep a fixed-point pose (position, velocity, angle, spin) and a reference shape they are redrawn from when the drawn pose changes, keeping their cell count. Gravity accelerates them (capped at 4 cells per tick); buoyancy and quadratic drag from the displaced fluid give terminal speeds (rock about 0.85 cells per tick in water, driftwood about 0.33 rising). A resting body whose centre of mass lies outside its contacts, or that sits on a single point, tips about the contact; bodies slide off a step, a fast body pushes sand and gravel aside (moved, never deleted) and embeds deeper than one set down gently, and a hit passes momentum to a free body. Bodies at rest sleep. Mechanisms in SPEC §2.1 *Rigid bodies*; code in `desktank_sim::world::bodies::{motion, shape}`. Tests in `crates/desktank-sim/tests/bodies.rs`.

- T6b plant individuals and species (D18, SPEC §2.7): every plant is an entry in a `BTreeMap` plant table (species, age, health, stored energy, part counts, base, bounds, parent, generation) whose id is in its cells' `state`; cells use the generic part materials root, stem and leaf, with the part-local position in the variant byte's low 6 bits. - T8 persistence: `desktank_sim::save` (OS-agnostic, builds for wasm32 for WB3) encodes a `World` as a 20-byte envelope (magic `DeskTank`, format version, reserved, payload length, CRC-32) around an LZ4 block of a `postcard` snapshot (`save::v1`, frozen types; `decode` dispatches on the version, so v2 adds a module and a migration). The snapshot holds everything that feeds later ticks: size, seed, tick, surface row; the grid as planes (material and state run-length encoded, colour noise XOR the fresh-tank noise); each chunk's pending work, changed rect and sleep timer; the body table with pose and reference shape and the next body id; the random-tick lists in scheduler order, cursor and budget; the nutrient, CO₂ and light fields with their settling flags; the plant table with its update bookkeeping, next id, cursor and cap; the creatures and next id; and every material and species id's name, checked on load. RNG streams are derived from (seed, tick, stream), so none are stored. Loads validate sizes, indices, poses and finite values (`SaveError`). `desktank_platform::SaveStore` keeps saves as `main-NNNNNNNNNN.dtank` in `data_dir()/tanks` (`DESKTANK_DATA_DIR` overrides): writes go to `main.tmp`, fsync, rename to the next generation, fsync the directory, then prune to the newest 3; ownership is an OS advisory lock (`File::try_lock`) on `main.lock`; a save that doesn't load is renamed `.dtank.damaged` and the next older one is tried. `desktank window` opens the saved tank (at its saved size) or a new starter scene, autosaves every 5 minutes from its existing wake-ups (no timers), saves when hidden and on exit (`exiting`, which also runs on macOS Quit), and with the tank owned elsewhere shows the newest save read-only; `--fresh` and `--no-save`; `--demo-pour` never saves. - T6c part 1, rosettes and carpets (SPEC §2.7 *Growth forms*): two new growth forms in `world/plants.rs`, parametrised by `[rosette]` / `[carpet]` in the species TOML (`crown`, `blades`, `blade_length`, `lean`, `root_depth`, `spacing`, `substrates`). A rosette roots a crown (stem cells, position 0) on the substrate that grows rightwards to `crown` cells (1–3); blades are leaf cells (no new material) that start above each crown cell and beside its ends (at most `crown + 2`) and grow up, those off the crown's centre stepping a cell outwards at their second cell and every `lean` cells after, more often the further out (to every third cell), so outer blades arch away; a blade's next cell follows from its position and column alone, so only its tip finds water.
